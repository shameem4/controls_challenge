How to read the drift. The benchmark scores lateral acceleration error, not position, so integrating that error freely over 40 s would show metres of wander the cost never penalises. The lane offset here is a leaky integral (≈3 s memory) of the heading error (a_achieved − a_commanded) / v — corner drift reads true, while long-run drift does not accumulate fictitiously. Costs shown are the real benchmark numbers from tinyphysics, steps 100–500.
